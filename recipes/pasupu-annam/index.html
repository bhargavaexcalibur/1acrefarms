Pasupu Annam (Andhra Turmeric Rice)

పసుపు అన్నం · हल्दी वाले चावल · हरिद्रौदनम् · Pasupu Annam · Haldi Wale Chawal

Pasupu annam is plain rice turned bright yellow with turmeric and tossed in a crackling tempering of mustard seeds, chana dal, curry leaves and green chillies, with fried cashews on top. Andhra homes make it for lunch boxes, for quick weekday meals and as a prasadam on puja days, often with the morning's leftover rice.

Our hill turmeric powder (Konda Pasupu) gives the rice its deep yellow colour and an earthy turmeric smell, so half a teaspoon is enough for 4. Whole cashews (Jeedipappu) fried in the same oil add the crunch.

  • Prep10 min
  • Cook25 min
  • Total50 min
  • Serves4

Total includes about 15 minutes for the cooked rice to cool.

In short

Cook rice and let it cool. Fry cashews, mustard seeds, chana dal, chillies and curry leaves in oil, add hill turmeric powder and toss in the rice.

Ingredients serves 4

For the rice

  • 1 cup rice sona masoori, about 200 g; makes about 3 cups cooked
  • 2 cups water for cooking the rice
  • 1 tsp salt or to taste

For the tempering

  • 3 tbsp oil groundnut or sesame oil
  • 15 cashews whole or halved
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 tbsp chana dal
  • 1 tsp urad dal
  • 2 dried red chillies broken in half
  • 2 green chillies slit lengthwise
  • 2 sprigs curry leaves about 20 leaves
  • 1 tsp grated ginger optional
  • a pinch of asafoetida (hing)
  • ½ tsp hill turmeric powder Konda Pasupu
  • 1 tbsp ghee (optional) stirred in at the end

Method

  1. Wash the rice in 2–3 changes of water and drain it.
  2. Cook the rice so the grains stay separate, then spread it on a plate to cool. Use 2 cups water to 1 cup rice in a pot or pressure cooker (2 whistles), and fluff it with a fork before spreading it out.
  3. Let the rice cool for about 15 minutes until it is just warm. Sprinkle the salt over it.
  4. Heat the oil in a wide kadai on medium heat.
  5. Fry the cashews in the oil till golden; lift out. Add mustard seeds and chana dal. Keep the cashews on a plate; they turn golden in about 1 minute.
  6. Once the mustard seeds splutter, add the urad dal and the dried red chillies and stir.
  7. When the dal turns golden, add the chillies and curry leaves, then the turmeric on low heat. Add the ginger and asafoetida with the curry leaves, and stir the turmeric for only 5–10 seconds.
  8. Switch off the heat straight away so the turmeric does not burn.
  9. Tip in the rice, cashews and salt and toss gently till evenly yellow. Use a flat spatula and lift and fold rather than pressing down, so the grains stay whole.
  10. Taste for salt, stir in the ghee if using, cover and leave for 5 minutes before serving so the rice takes on the tempering.

Tips

  • Spread the rice to cool first so the grains do not break.
  • Add the turmeric on low heat and stir for only a few seconds; burnt turmeric turns the rice bitter.
  • Hill turmeric powder colours strongly; start with ½ tsp for 1 cup of rice and add a pinch more only if you want a deeper yellow.
  • Leftover rice from the morning works well, as long as the grains are separate.

Variations

  • Turmeric powder: our everyday Pasupu turmeric powder works the same way; use the same ½ tsp.
  • Peanuts: fry 2 tbsp raw peanuts in the oil before the cashews for extra crunch.
  • Lemon: squeeze in the juice of half a lemon with the rice for a tangy, pulihora-style rice.
  • Coconut: toss in 2 tbsp grated coconut along with the rice.

Serving

Serve warm or at room temperature with curd, appadalu or a dry potato fry. It also packs well for lunch boxes and travel.

Storage

Best eaten the same day. Keep leftovers covered in the fridge for up to 1 day and warm them with a sprinkle of water.

Questions

How do you make pasupu annam?

Cook rice so the grains stay separate and let it cool. Fry cashews, mustard seeds, chana dal, chillies and curry leaves in oil, add turmeric on low heat for a few seconds, then toss in the rice with salt.

How much turmeric powder goes into turmeric rice?

About ½ tsp for 1 cup of raw rice (about 3 cups cooked). Hill turmeric powder colours strongly, so start with less and add a pinch more if needed.

Why is my turmeric rice bitter?

The turmeric burnt in the hot oil, or too much went in. Add it on low heat, stir for only a few seconds and switch off before tipping in the rice.

Can I make pasupu annam with leftover rice?

Yes. Leftover rice with separate grains works well. Break up any lumps with your fingers before tossing it in the tempering.
