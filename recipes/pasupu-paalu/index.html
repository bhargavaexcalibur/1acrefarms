Warm Pasupu Paalu (Turmeric Milk)

పసుపు పాలు · हल्दी वाला दूध · Pasupu Paalu · Haldi Wala Doodh

Karthika Somavaram evening: a warm cup of pasupu paalu. Simmer milk with turmeric and a pinch of pepper, switch off, then stir in a spoon of jaggery.

పాలలో పసుపు, చిటికెడు మిరియాల పొడి వేసి మరిగించి, స్టవ్ ఆపాక చెంచా బెల్లం కలపండి.

दूध में हल्दी और चुटकी भर काली मिर्च उबालें, आँच बंद करके एक चम्मच गुड़ मिलाएँ।

  • Time5 minutes
  • Makes1 cup
  • Made withTurmeric Powder · Hill Turmeric Powder

Ingredients

  • 1 cup milk
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • A pinch of black pepper
  • 1 tsp grated palm or cane jaggery

Method

  1. Warm the milk with the turmeric and pepper; simmer 2 minutes.
  2. Switch off and wait a minute.
  3. Stir in the jaggery and serve warm.

TipStrain through a fine sieve if you like it smooth.
