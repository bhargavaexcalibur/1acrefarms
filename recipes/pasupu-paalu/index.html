Warm Pasupu Paalu (Turmeric Milk)

పసుపు పాలు · हल्दी वाला दूध · Pasupu Paalu · Haldi Wala Doodh

Pasupu paalu is warm milk simmered with a little turmeric and a pinch of black pepper, then sweetened with jaggery. It is an everyday cup in many Telugu homes, made on cool evenings and often through Karthika masam, the month of Somavaram (Monday) pujas and evening lamps.

The turmeric turns the milk golden and gives it an earthy, slightly bitter edge, the pepper adds a little heat, and the jaggery rounds it off with a caramel sweetness. This recipe makes one cup in about 5 minutes.

  • Prep1 min
  • Cook4 min
  • Total5 min
  • Makes1 cup

In short

Simmer milk with turmeric and a pinch of pepper, switch off, then stir in a spoon of jaggery.

Ingredients makes 1 cup

  • 1 cup milk about 240 ml
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder Turmeric Powder or Hill Turmeric Powder
  • A pinch of black pepper powdered or crushed
  • 1 tsp grated palm or cane jaggery or to taste
  • A pinch of cardamom powder (optional)

Method

  1. Pour the milk into a small steel saucepan.
  2. Add the turmeric and pepper (and the cardamom, if using) and stir until no specks of turmeric float on top. They mix in more evenly before the milk is heated.
  3. Set the pan on medium-low heat. Warm the milk with the turmeric and pepper; simmer 2 minutes. Once small bubbles appear around the edge, turn the heat to low and let it bubble gently.
  4. Stir now and then and keep an eye on the pan, as milk rises fast once it boils. If it starts to climb, lift the pan off the heat for a few seconds.
  5. It is ready when the milk is an even golden yellow and the raw smell of turmeric has gone.
  6. Switch off and wait a minute. The milk drops just below the boil, so the jaggery will not split it.
  7. For a smooth cup, pour the milk through a fine sieve into a tumbler or mug.
  8. Stir in the jaggery and serve warm. Stir for 20–30 seconds, until it dissolves, and taste for sweetness.

Tips

  • Strain through a fine sieve if you like it smooth.
  • Add the jaggery only after switching off; jaggery stirred into boiling milk can split it.
  • Use a steel pan and spoon; turmeric stains plastic and wood.
  • Making more? Use the same amounts for each cup of milk.

Variations

  • Hill turmeric: use Hill Turmeric Powder (konda pasupu) in place of turmeric powder, in the same amount.
  • Palm or cane jaggery: palm jaggery gives a deeper, slightly smoky sweetness; cane jaggery a lighter, caramel one.
  • Milder cup: leave out the pepper and add a pinch of cardamom powder instead.

Serving

Serve warm in a tumbler or mug, on a cool evening or after the Karthika Somavaram puja.

Storage

Make it as you need it and drink it warm. Reheating after the jaggery is in can split the milk.

Questions

Why did my pasupu paalu curdle?

Jaggery stirred into milk that is still boiling can make it split. Switch off the heat, wait about a minute, then stir in the grated jaggery.

How much turmeric should I use for one cup of milk?

¼ tsp turmeric powder for 1 cup (about 240 ml) of milk. More than that makes the taste sharp and earthy.

Can I skip the black pepper?

Yes. The pepper adds a little heat to the taste; leave it out, or use a pinch of cardamom instead, for a milder cup.

Should I use palm jaggery or cane jaggery?

Either works, 1 tsp grated per cup. Palm jaggery tastes deeper and slightly smoky; cane jaggery is lighter and more like caramel.
