Where it comes from

Seven packs, from across Andhra.

Each pack comes from the place it is known for, and all seven are packed in Narasaraopet, Palnadu.

Palnadu & Guntur

Turmeric powder (pasupu), and Sannam and Teja chilli powders (kaaram).

Araku & Maredumilli hills

Hill turmeric (konda pasupu), packed as fine powder.

Chittoor & Anakapalli

Cane jaggery (cheruku bellam), dark and slow-boiled.

Godavari delta

Palm jaggery (thati bellam), made from palmyra sap.

Chirala, Bapatla & Vizag coast

Cashew kernels (jeedipappu), whole, plain and unsalted.

Our story

One acre, and an honest promise.

1acrefarms began with a single acre and a simple belief: food — and the home it feeds — is better when it's grown in living soil, kept simple, and travels a short, honest distance.

We grow what we can on our own acre, and for everything else we go straight to the people who do it right — small hill farmers rebuilding their soil, and tribal families in the hills who gather from the forest the way they always have. We clean it and pack it under our name.

Farm food from village makers and honest home care — from the soil, to us, to you.
