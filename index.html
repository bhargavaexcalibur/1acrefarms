Our story

One acre, and an honest promise.

1acrefarms began with a single acre and a simple belief: food — and the home it feeds — is better when it's grown in living soil, kept simple, and travels a short, honest distance.

We grow what we can on our own acre, and for everything else we go straight to the people who do it right — small hill farmers rebuilding their soil, and tribal families in the hills who gather from the forest the way they always have. We clean it, pack it under our name, and bring it to your door.

Farm food from village makers, wellness and honest home care — from the soil, to us, to you.
