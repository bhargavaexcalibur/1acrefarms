Cane jaggery or palm jaggery: which to use

చెరుకు బెల్లం · తాటి బెల్లం · गन्ने का गुड़ · ताड़ का गुड़ · इक्षुगुडः · तालगुडः

Bellam in a Telugu kitchen usually means one of two things: cheruku bellam, made from sugarcane juice, or thati bellam, made from the sap of the palmyra palm. FSSAI treats them as two separate products, each with its own standard.

They can look alike in a lump, but they taste different and suit different dishes. Here is how to tell them apart, which to use where, and how to keep them. There is a full Telugu section further down.

In short

Cane jaggery (cheruku bellam) is boiled-down sugarcane juice with a mellow, caramel sweetness: the one for payasam, pongal and chimmili. Palm jaggery (thati bellam) is boiled-down palmyra sap, darker and deeper in taste: a favourite in filter coffee and tea. You can swap one for the other gram for gram; start with a little less palm jaggery. Keep both in an airtight jar.

Where they come from

Cane jaggery is made wherever sugarcane grows: the juice is pressed out of the cane and boiled down in open pans. In Andhra Pradesh, Anakapalli has the second largest jaggery market in India.

Palm jaggery is made from the sap of the palmyra palm, thati chettu in Telugu. The sap is called neera, or thati neera, and it is boiled down until it sets.

Which one to use

  • Payasam, sweet pongal and bellam paramannam: cane jaggery.
  • Chimmili, chikki and laddu: cane jaggery.
  • Filter coffee and tea: palm jaggery. Its deeper taste stands up to the decoction; try our palm jaggery coffee.
  • Pasupu paalu: either one, as you like it.

Swapping one for the other

Use the same weight. Palm jaggery tastes stronger, so start with a little less and add more to taste.

In milk, coffee or tea, add either jaggery after the milk is off the boil, so the milk does not split.

Keeping it well

  • Keep jaggery in an airtight jar in a cool, dry place.
  • Use a dry spoon or knife. Jaggery takes in moisture from the air and turns sticky.
  • If it sets hard, grate it.
  • Use it within the best-before date on the pack.

Questions

Is palm jaggery the same as karupatti?

Yes. Karupatti is the Tamil name for jaggery made from palmyra sap. In Telugu it is thati bellam, and in Hindi taad ka gud.

Is gud the same as jaggery?

Yes. Gud, or gur, is the Hindi word for jaggery, and bellam is the Telugu one. On its own it usually means cane jaggery.

Why has my jaggery gone sticky?

It has taken in moisture from the air, which happens faster in humid weather. Move it to an airtight jar and use a dry spoon.

Can I use palm jaggery in payasam?

Yes. It gives a darker colour and a deeper taste. Add it off the heat, as you would cane jaggery.
