Cancellation

  • You can cancel any time before dispatch by messaging us on WhatsApp. If you have already paid, we refund the full amount. We never charge a cancellation fee.

Returns and refunds

Our products are food, so for hygiene and safety we cannot take back a pack that was delivered in good condition and has been opened, just because you changed your mind. In every case below, we offer a free replacement or a full refund, as you prefer.

Problems on arrival

The pack is damaged, torn or leaking, you received the wrong product or quantity, or an item is missing. Message us on WhatsApp (+91 78999 29779) within 48 hours of delivery, with your order details and photos of the pack and the outer packaging. An unboxing video helps us settle it faster.

Quality problems

The product is not as described or has a quality problem you find on opening (for example insects, mould, foreign matter or an off smell), or it is past its best-before date on delivery. Tell us any time before the best-before date, with photos of the product and of the batch number printed on the pack.

Late delivery

If your order arrives later than the delivery date we gave you, you can refuse it or ask for a full refund, as explained under Shipping and delivery.

Return shipping and refunds

You never pay for return shipping. Usually we do not need the pack back. If we do, we arrange the pickup at our cost.

Refunds are made to the original payment method, or by bank transfer or UPI for pay-on-delivery orders, within 7 working days of approval.

Orders placed through Amazon, Meesho or other marketplaces follow that marketplace's return and refund policy.

Payment

  • All prices are in Indian Rupees and include GST. The MRP is printed on every pack. We never charge above MRP.
  • How you can pay: by UPI or bank transfer to our business account before dispatch, or pay on delivery where it is available.
  • Where pay on delivery is available: for orders up to ₹999 in South India (Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala and Puducherry), and up to ₹599 elsewhere in India. Larger orders are paid by UPI before dispatch. Pay on delivery is not available in the North-East, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Sikkim, Andaman & Nicobar and Lakshadweep, or at pincodes our couriers do not serve for pay on delivery. If an earlier pay-on-delivery order was refused at the door, we may ask for payment by UPI. The cart shows which options apply as soon as you enter your pincode, before you send the order.
  • Fees: there is no extra fee for any payment method, and delivery is free.
  • Security: UPI and bank payments are made inside your own bank or UPI app, so we never see your PIN, password or card details. We will never ask for a card number, PIN or OTP over WhatsApp or phone.
  • No automatic payments: we do not take subscriptions or repeat payments, so there is nothing to cancel.
  • If a payment goes wrong: contact us first and we will sort it out. You can also raise a dispute in your UPI app or with your bank. For pay-on-delivery orders, the courier delivering your order collects the payment on our behalf.
  • We send a GST tax invoice with every order.

Privacy

What we collect: when you order or message us, we collect your name, phone number, delivery address, order details and the messages you send. If we refund you by bank transfer or UPI, we also ask for those details.

Why: only to confirm, pack, deliver and support your order, to issue your GST invoice, and to process refunds. With your consent, we may also send occasional WhatsApp updates about new products; reply STOP at any time and we will stop.

Who we share it with: the courier delivering your order; the banks and payment providers handling your payment; the service providers we use to run our business, such as WhatsApp (Meta) for order chats and Google for email and our order records; and government authorities when the law requires it, for example for GST. We do not sell or rent your data.

Marketplace orders: for orders on Amazon, Meesho and other marketplaces, the marketplace collects your data under its own privacy policy and shares with us only what is needed to deliver your order.

On this website: we do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your shopping cart and delivery pincode are saved only in your own browser.

How we protect it: your data is kept only in our own password-protected business accounts, and only the proprietor handles it.

How long we keep it: order and invoice records are kept for as long as GST law requires (currently 72 months after the due date of the annual return). Other personal data is deleted once it is no longer needed.

Your rights: under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent to updates, by writing to the grievance officer above. We reply within one month. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
