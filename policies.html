Returns and refunds

Our products are food, so for hygiene and safety we cannot take back a pack that has been delivered in good condition and opened. We will make it right when:

  • the pack arrives damaged, torn or leaking;
  • you receive the wrong product or quantity, or an item is missing;
  • the pack is past its best-before date on delivery.

How to claim: message us on WhatsApp (+91 78999 29779) within 48 hours of delivery, with your order details and clear photos of the pack and the outer packaging. An unboxing video helps us settle claims faster. We will offer a free replacement or a full refund, as you prefer.

Refunds are made to the original payment method, or by bank transfer or UPI for cash-on-delivery orders, within 7 working days of approval.

Orders placed through Amazon, Meesho or other marketplaces follow that marketplace's return and refund policy.
