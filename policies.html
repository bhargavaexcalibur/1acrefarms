Cancellation, returns and refunds

Cancellation

  • You can cancel any time before dispatch by messaging us on WhatsApp or by email. We refund the full amount. We never charge a cancellation fee.
  • After dispatch, an order cannot be cancelled. If something is wrong when it arrives, see Returns below.

Returns

Our products are food, so for hygiene we cannot take back a pack once it has been delivered, and we do not take change-of-mind returns. If something is wrong with your order, we put it right with a free replacement or a full refund, as you prefer:

Damaged, leaking or wrong pack

The pack arrives damaged, torn or leaking, you received the wrong product or quantity, or an item is missing. Send us a photo or video on WhatsApp (+91 78999 29779) or by email (1acrefarmsindia@gmail.com) within 48 hours of delivery, with your order details. Photos of the pack and the outer packaging, or an unboxing video, help us settle it faster.

Quality problems

The product is not as described or has a quality problem you find on opening (for example insects, mould, foreign matter or an off smell), or it is past its best-before date on delivery. Tell us any time before the best-before date, with photos of the product and of the batch number printed on the pack.

Late delivery

If your order arrives later than the delivery date we gave you, you can refuse it or ask for a full refund, as explained under Shipping and delivery.

Parcels that come back to us

If a parcel comes back to us because the address was wrong or incomplete, or because the delivery was refused (other than a late delivery), we contact you to send it again at no extra cost, or we refund the full amount you paid.

Return shipping

You never pay for return shipping on a damaged, wrong or faulty pack. Usually we do not need the pack back. If we do, we arrange the pickup at our cost.

Refunds

Refunds go to the original payment method (the same UPI ID, card, bank account or wallet you paid with) within 5–7 working days after we approve them. Your bank or card issuer may take a few more days to show it in your account.

Orders placed through Amazon, Meesho or other marketplaces follow that marketplace's return and refund policy.

Privacy

What we collect: when you order or message us, we collect your name, phone number, delivery address, order details and the messages you send. If we refund you by bank transfer or UPI, we also ask for those details.

Why: only to confirm, pack, deliver and support your order, to issue your GST invoice, and to process refunds. With your consent, we may also send occasional WhatsApp updates about new products; reply STOP at any time and we will stop.

Who we share it with: the courier delivering your order; the banks and payment providers handling your payment; the service providers we use to run our business, such as WhatsApp (Meta) for order chats and Google for email and our order records; and government authorities when the law requires it, for example for GST. We do not sell or rent your data.

Direct orders and payments: for an order placed directly with us, we collect your name, phone number, email address and delivery address, to deliver your order and to issue your GST invoice. Our payment processors (Razorpay, and Cashfree as a backup) and our courier partners (booked through Shiprocket) receive only what they need to take your payment and deliver your parcel. We do not sell your data.

Marketplace orders: for orders on Amazon, Meesho and other marketplaces, the marketplace collects your data under its own privacy policy and shares with us only what is needed to deliver your order.

On this website: we use Google Analytics to count visits and to see which pages are read and which links are clicked, such as “Buy on Amazon”. It sets cookies in your browser and gives us totals only: we never see your name, phone number or address, and Google Analytics does not store IP addresses. We do not use advertising cookies. The website also saves small settings, such as a closed language banner, in your own browser.

How we protect it: your data is kept only in our own password-protected business accounts, and only the proprietor handles it.

How long we keep it: order and invoice records are kept for as long as GST law requires (currently 72 months after the due date of the annual return). Other personal data is deleted once it is no longer needed.

Your rights: under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask what data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent to updates, by writing to the grievance officer above. We reply within one month. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
